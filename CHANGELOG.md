## 0.1.0

- Extracted Endcrawl's AVFoundation and MediaCodec video encoders.
- Added a generic Dart frame/session API, output validation and native capability probes.
- Added cancellable music muxing, looping/trimming and fade-out.
- Preserve video-track timing through audio muxing, including fractional-millisecond ends.
- Added iOS CocoaPods/SPM integration, Android plugin registration, example and tests.
- Integrated locally into Endcrawl and Starcrawler; publication remains disabled.
